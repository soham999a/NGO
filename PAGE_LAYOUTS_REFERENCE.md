## 🎨 DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### Spacing
- Section padding: 60-80px top/bottom
- Element spacing: 20-30px between items
- Card spacing: 15-20px gap

### Colors
- **Headers**: Dark blue (#2874A6) or black
- **Body text**: Dark gray (#333333)
- **Buttons**: Orange (#FF8C00) or Blue (#2874A6)
- **Backgrounds**: White or light gray (#F5F5F5)

### Typography
- **H1 (Page titles)**: 42-48px, Bold
- **H2 (Section titles)**: 32-36px, Bold
- **H3 (Card titles)**: 24-28px, Semi-bold
- **Body text**: 16-18px, Regular
- **Button text**: 16px, Semi-bold

### Images
- **Hero images**: Full width, 600-800px height
- **Card images**: Square or 16:9 ratio
- **Gallery images**: Consistent size, 300-400px

### Buttons
- **Primary (Orange)**: Call-to-action (Donate, Apply, Join)
- **Secondary (Blue)**: Navigation (Learn More, Read More)
- **Size**: 48px height minimum (for mobile)
- **Padding**: 20-30px horizontal

---

## 📱 MOBILE CONSIDERATIONS

On mobile, stack elements vertically:
- 4 cards in a row → 1 card per row
- Side-by-side content → Stack vertically
- Reduce font sizes by 20-30%
- Increase button sizes for touch
- Reduce padding to save space

---

## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Consistency is key**: Use same card style across all pages
2. **White space**: Don't cram everything together
3. **Visual hierarchy**: Bigger = more important
4. **Call-to-action**: Every page should have a clear next step
5. **Images**: Use high-quality, relevant photos
6. **Mobile first**: Always check mobile view
