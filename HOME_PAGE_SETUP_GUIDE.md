## 🎨 STYLING TIPS

1. **Use your brand colors consistently:**
   - Buttons: Orange (#FF8C00) and Blue (#2874A6)
   - Headings: Dark blue
   - Backgrounds: White or light gray

2. **Fonts:**
   - Headings: Bold, 36-48px
   - Body text: Regular, 16-18px
   - Keep it readable!

3. **Spacing:**
   - Add padding between sections (50-80px)
   - Keep cards evenly spaced

---

## ✅ AFTER ADDING ELEMENTS

1. Save your page in Wix Editor
2. Click "Test Site" to preview
3. The code will automatically:
   - Animate hero section on load
   - Count up impact numbers when you scroll
   - Add hover effects to buttons and cards
   - Enable click navigation to other pages
   - Make gallery images clickable
